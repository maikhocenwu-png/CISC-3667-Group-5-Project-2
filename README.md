This is a game inspired by Geo Guesser!

How to play: 
1. **Pick a length:** Quick (5 rounds) or Regular (10 rounds).
2. **Pick a difficulty:**
   - **Easy:** choose the landmark from 4 options.
   - **Hard:** type the landmark's name corretly & Map has no countries name.
3. **Guess the landmark:** look at the photo and guess the landmark correctly.
4. **Pin it on the map:** Guess the location of the landmark, click to pin where the landmark is on the world map, then press to confirm the location. Then you'll get point base on the location you pin, the closer you are, the more points you get.
5. **Learn more about it:** read a fun fact and a short history of the landmark.
6. After the last round you see your final score.

How to run/install the game:
1. Clone the repository
2. Open 'index.html' in a browser
